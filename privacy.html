Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

This policy explains how the Nimbus Noir weather application (the “App”) handles information. The App does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Location and searches

If you allow location access, the App uses your current latitude and longitude to request local weather, weather alerts, and a readable place name. You can deny or revoke location access and search for a city instead. Location is requested only while you use the App; the App does not request background location.

Third-party services

To provide its features, the App sends weather or place queries to Open-Meteo, the U.S. National Weather Service, BigDataCloud, and RainViewer. Radar map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap and RainViewer. Depending on the feature used, these requests can include coordinates, a city-search term, and standard network information such as an IP address. These independent providers may process or retain information under their own policies and terms.

  • Open-Meteo terms and privacy information
  • National Weather Service privacy policy
  • BigDataCloud privacy policy
  • RainViewer privacy policy
  • OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy

National Weather Service observations from nearby stations may be used for current conditions. Your selected coordinates are used to find nearby stations; their distance and observation time are displayed in the App.

Data stored on your device

Your unit preference, pinned locations, last selected location, and cached forecasts are stored locally on your device. The App does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives or stores this information. You can remove locally stored information by deleting the App or clearing its website data.

Sharing

The Share button opens the system share sheet with a weather summary. Nothing is shared unless you choose a destination and confirm it.

Children

The App is a general-audience weather utility and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other account information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the App or its service providers change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the Nimbus Noir support page.
