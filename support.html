Nimbus Noir Support

Get help with location access, forecasts, radar, saved locations, sharing, or another problem with Nimbus Noir.

Contact support

Open a support request

Support requests are handled through the project’s public GitHub issue tracker. Do not include your precise location, address, Apple ID, passwords, or other sensitive information.

Common fixes

Location is unavailable

Open iPhone Settings, find Nimbus Noir, select Location, and choose “While Using the App.” You can also deny location access and search for a city manually.

The forecast or radar will not load

Check your internet connection and try again. Previously viewed forecasts may remain available from the local cache, but live forecasts, alerts, place searches, and radar require network access.

Reset saved information

Deleting Nimbus Noir removes its locally stored preferences, pinned locations, last selected location, and cached forecasts. Reinstalling starts with a clean local store.

Service information

Nimbus Noir is a weather-information utility. Do not rely on it as your only source for emergency warnings. Follow instructions from local authorities and official weather services during hazardous conditions.
